At a party and need to learn Oops, All In right now? Grab your own phone and type in the four-letter room code on the website. Everyone starts with 100 pretend chips. Every round features a quick mini game. But before you actually play, you bet on yourself. When it is your turn, you can stay in to match, raise or fold. Do not worry about losing everything right away. In rounds one and two, bets are capped at 20 chips. But starting in round three, the betting limits disappear. You can push every single chip you have and go Oops, All In. Some mini games use judges to pick the best answer. Judges just vote. They never bet on that round. Whoever has the most chips at the end of the game wins. Pro tip: start in Minigames mode to learn without any chips first. Now grab your phone, type in that room code and go take the pot.
