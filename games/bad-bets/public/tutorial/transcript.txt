Join the room
One person starts a game. Everyone else joins with the room code, on their own device.

100 chips. 9 rounds.
In party mode, everyone starts with one hundred chips. Have the most after nine rounds to win.

Pick your bet
Most rounds include everyone. Choose five, ten, or twenty chips before you know which game you will play.

What happens to your chips?
Bet ten from a balance of one hundred. Win, and finish with one hundred ten. Lose, and finish with ninety. A draw gives your bet back.

Spotlight rounds
Rounds three, six, and nine are spotlight rounds. Stay in as the entry amount rises, or step out without paying the entry.

The audience has a role
The audience votes for creative entries. In Ballpark, they can bet five chips on a team. Judges cannot bet on the result they decide.

Choose the game lineup
The host chooses the games. If game bans are on and three or more games are eligible, everyone votes to remove one option for that round.

Everyone stays in the game
Low on chips? If you have fewer than five, you start the next round with five. No one is eliminated. After round nine, the most chips wins.
