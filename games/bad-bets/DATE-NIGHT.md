# Date Night — September 24, 2026

Implemented locally: Start a game → Date Night, or select Date Night in the lobby. Exactly two players. 36 original spoken prompts, twelve in each level: Keep it light, A little more, Closer together. Alternate who starts. No chips, answer scoring, timer, or typed personal answers.

Both players independently agree at the two depth transitions. Either may skip a question or finish without a penalty. The host cannot force an early answer reveal. The closing screen offers an optional quiet moment of eye contact, with no required duration. End anytime; 20–45 minutes is a planning estimate, not a measured session length.

This is an adaptation inspired by reciprocal, gradual self-disclosure, not a reproduction of the studied 36-question instrument. Research on that instrument does not establish that these original prompts work, guarantee romance, or prove a neurological “seal” of trust. Four minutes of eye contact is not presented as a required part of the 1997 procedure.

References reviewed: user-supplied A More Beautiful Question interview, Greater Good in Action practice, Lemonade article, and Psych Central article. Applied the gradual sequence, alternating answers, listening, and mutual comfort. Excluded guaranteed-love claims, hormone explanations, forced disclosure, and mandatory eye contact.

Research: Aron et al. (1997), The Experimental Generation of Interpersonal Closeness: A Procedure and Some Preliminary Findings. https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167297234003
Practice and instructions: https://ggia.berkeley.edu/practice/36_questions_for_increasing_closeness

Validation: 82 automated tests pass, including full 36-card progression, consent gates, duplicate consent, two-player restriction, no typed personal answers, unchanged chips, and either-player exit. Browser/device playtesting and public deployment of this change are not yet verified.
