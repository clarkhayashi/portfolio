# Date Night — full current question list

Review copy. These are the 36 original prompts currently implemented, not the original Aron questionnaire. The current tone is gentle and reflective; prompts have not been rewritten in this preview update. Mark each KEEP / REWRITE / CUT.

## Keep it light

1. What little ritual makes you look forward to an ordinary evening?
2. Which place brings out your most playful side?
3. What hobby would you enjoy being hilariously bad at together?
4. What food has a story attached to it for you?
5. What tiny thing did someone notice about you that made you smile?
6. What would you put in a care package for your past self?
7. Which version of you shows up around your oldest friends?
8. What is a small adventure you would actually say yes to this week?
9. What part of your routine would you like someone to understand better?
10. What have you changed your mind about in a good way lately?
11. What makes a conversation feel easy for you?
12. What is something from this conversation you would like to hear more about?

## A little more

13. What quiet decision turned out to matter more than you expected?
14. What did you once find difficult that now feels natural?
15. What is a tradition you want to keep, change, or invent?
16. When has someone helped you feel capable without taking over?
17. What part of your life would you like to make more room for?
18. What is a memory you would describe through sounds or smells?
19. What expectation have you given yourself permission to let go of?
20. What kind of encouragement works for you when you are unsure?
21. What would trying something new look like if nobody graded you?
22. What does a good apology look like in everyday life to you?
23. What is a small risk you are glad you took?
24. What did you learn about the other person that surprised you today?

## Closer together

25. What helps you say that something is not working for you?
26. When you go quiet, what would you like the other person to check rather than assume?
27. What would feeling supported look like in the coming week?
28. What part of yourself do you want to make less effort to hide?
29. What boundary makes it easier for you to relax with someone?
30. How would you like us to handle a misunderstanding before it grows?
31. What is something you are still figuring out and do not need an answer for yet?
32. What have you appreciated about how the other person listened today?
33. What helps you feel cared for without feeling managed?
34. What would you like us to ask each other more often?
35. What is one small thing we could try together without promising a big outcome?
36. What would you like to carry from this conversation into the next time we meet?

## Proposed tone direction for the next draft

More playful specifics, fewer abstract self-improvement questions. Keep deeper prompts optional. Examples to react to:

- We have $20 and two hours to turn a boring Tuesday into a date. What’s the plan?
- Which of your tiny habits would make me say “oh, you’re THAT person”?
- What would our wildly unqualified two-person business sell?
- What’s your oddly specific green flag?
- What’s a small moment between us you’d replay?
- When you’re having a rough day, what should I do—and what should I absolutely not do?
